THREE BAR SYSTEM

ULT BAR
  • Gain: M1 hits +2-3%, E/R skills +5-8%, Black Flash +15%
  • Loss: ONLY during Ult (becomes timer)
  • Fill Rates: Fast 30-40s (Yuji/Maki/Choso), Medium 45-55s (Megumi/Hakari), Slow 60-75s (Gojo/Kashimo/Yuta)
  • Design: Pure reward for aggression, effort never wasted
EVASIVE BAR
  • Gain: Taking damage +2-3%/hit, blocked +0.5%, combos +4-6%
  • Loss: 100% on Evasive Dash use
  • Usage: ONLY when STUNNED (not in animations)
  • Recovery: 10-15f vulnerability after dash
  • Design: One escape per engagement, anti-combo insurance
R SPECIAL
  • CD Tiers: Instant (Todo/Naoya), Low 1-3s (Yuji/Choso), Medium 5-8s (Megumi/Hakari), High 10-15s+ (Gojo/Mahito)
  • Design: Cooldown-based tactical tool, no resource cost
  • Visibility: Icon above enemy HP bar, glows when ready
  • Audio: Character-specific CD completion sound

Balance Safeguards
  • Blue pull can be evasive-dashed out of (frame 8+)
  • Red orb has minimum 8 stud range (no point-blank safe poke)
  • Rapid Punches: 6-8 stud max range, no tracking
  • All teleports have 5f visual tell (blue shimmer)
  • Levitate Red requires full combo confirm (not raw usable)
  • Domain has 3.5s vulnerable startup
  • 15s burnout prevents ult farming
  • Infinity only reduces chip by 20% (not immunity)
Counterplay Guide
vs Base
  • Evasive dash on Blue pull frame 8+
  • Sidestep Rapid Punches (no tracking)
  • Punish Red orb whiff (12f recovery)
  • Stay outside 15 stud pull range
vs R Combos
  • Bait teleport, punish 10f recovery
  • Watch for 5f blue shimmer tell
  • Evasive after knocked up (denies 1+2+R)
  • Block Red explosion (chip only)
vs Domain
  • Burst during 3.5s startup
  • Kite to deny Hollow Purple aim
  • Survive until timer (2m30s)
  • Punish 15s burnout hard
